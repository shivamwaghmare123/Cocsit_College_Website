About Our College

Empowering Students Through Technology

The College of Computer Science & Information Technology (COCSIT), Latur, is committed to providing quality education in Computer Science, Information Technology and related fields.

Our approach combines academic learning with practical exposure, helping students develop technical knowledge, problem-solving abilities and the confidence required for a changing technology landscape.

We aim to create a learning environment where students can explore new ideas, develop their skills and prepare themselves for future academic and professional opportunities.

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in Computer Science and Information Technology, producing globally competent professionals with strong ethical values, technical knowledge and innovative skills.

Our Mission

  • Provide quality education that combines theoretical knowledge with practical exposure.
  • Encourage research, innovation and creativity in technology.
  • Promote industry-oriented learning and professional development.
  • Support holistic development and leadership qualities among students.
